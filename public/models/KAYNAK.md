# Deneyap Kart 1A v2 3B modeli / 3D model of the Deneyap Kart 1A v2

`deneyap-1a-v2-renkli.glb`: T3 Vakfı Deneyap Kart projesinin resmi 3B modelinden (`docs/3B Model (.step).STEP`, https://github.com/deneyapkart/deneyapkart-arduino-core) `tools/step2glb.mjs` ile üretilmiş, sadeleştirilmiş ve renkli glTF dosyası. Deneyap adı ve kart tasarımı T3 Vakfı / TÜBİTAK'a aittir.

`deneyap-1a-v2-renkli.glb`: a simplified, colored glTF converted with `tools/step2glb.mjs` from the official 3D model published by the T3 Foundation's Deneyap Kart project (`docs/3B Model (.step).STEP`, https://github.com/deneyapkart/deneyapkart-arduino-core). Deneyap and the board design belong to the T3 Foundation / TÜBİTAK.
